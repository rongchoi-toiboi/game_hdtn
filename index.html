How to play

Players alternate. On your turn, press on a spot and drag to another spot (or back to the same spot to make a loop). The curve may not cross any line, itself included, or pass through another spot. A new spot is then placed at the middle of your curve.

The board is endless. Scroll or pinch to zoom, and drag empty space to move the view. Lines are elastic: between moves they pull straight and push each other apart, so the drawing keeps rearranging itself and never clogs.

Each spot can have at most 3 lines. The number inside a spot is how many it can still take; a filled spot is full. The player who cannot move loses.

Both players start in the lobby: choose the rules, then each press Ready. Once both are ready the match begins, a quick draw picks who moves first, and names and rules stay locked. The host can set the clock to any number of minutes (0 for no limit). Each player has a clock that runs only on their turn, and running out of time loses the game. Use the Dark mode button at the top to switch themes.

Play on two devices

Host presses Create room and shares the 6-character code (or link). The other player types the code and presses Join room. That's it: the two devices find each other and connect. The host is player 1 and chooses the rules; once connected you meet in the lobby and both press Ready.

Connect manually (no relay needed)

If the relay is unreachable, Host creates an invite (a link when this page is online, otherwise a code) and copies it. Send it over; the other player opens the link or presses Paste. They send their reply back and the host presses Paste.
